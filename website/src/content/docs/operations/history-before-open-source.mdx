This document shares the development history of Actionbase before it was open-sourced. After open-sourcing, development history will be transparently available through public repositories and releases. This document provides insight into the journey that led to Actionbase's open-source release.

**Note:** This history covers the internal development period before open-sourcing. Future development will be transparently tracked in public repositories.

## MVP Phase

The MVP phase represents the entire period from project inception to open-source release. During this extended period, Actionbase was applied to various services and evolved through real-world challenges, demonstrating its viability and establishing a sustainable foundation.

### Phase 1: Foundation Building (April 2023)

**Focus:** Validating technical feasibility and establishing a robust technical foundation.

This extended period was invested to ensure a solid technical base that would support stable development in subsequent phases.

Key milestones:
- Project kickoff and initial planning
- Core design and implementation

### Phase 2: Service Integration (March 2024)

**Focus:** Validating through real-world service deployment.

The system was safely integrated into production by maintaining the existing system, applying dual writes, and gradually migrating read functionality after sufficient validation.

Key activities:
- First production service write/read migration

### Phase 3: Stabilization (July 2024)

**Focus:** System stabilization and tenant expansion.

Starting from a multi-tenant architecture, this phase introduced support for independent tenant configurations to ensure service isolation and enable isolated deployments.

This phase established a sustainable operational foundation.

Key improvements:
- System stabilization and tenant expansion
- Support for independent tenant configurations to isolate services
- Operations automation and monitoring enhancement
- Applied across KakaoShopping

### Phase 4: Building Sustainable Operations (January 2025)

**Focus:** Improving operational efficiency and developer experience, addressing technical debt.

During this phase, Actionbase continued to be applied to various services while improving its operational foundation and addressing technical debt.

### Phase 5: Improving Sustainability and Company-wide Adoption (May 2025 - December 2025)

**Focus:** Improving sustainability and expanding adoption across Kakao's major services.

This phase focused on addressing technical debt and improving long-term sustainability. The reimplementation process led to the decision to open-source Actionbase. While legacy code remains, the reimplementation continues to progress toward a more maintainable codebase. During this period, Actionbase expanded its adoption across Kakao's major services including KakaoShopping and KakaoTalk, demonstrating its scalability and reliability at a company-wide scale.

Key changes:
- Maintained API compatibility with existing systems
- Redesigned API structure
- Repository separation and organization
- Separated internal and open-source areas
- Expanded adoption to major services in KakaoShopping and KakaoTalk

## Open Source Release (January 2026)

After this extended MVP period, during which Actionbase was applied to various services and proven its viability, Actionbase was open-sourced in January 2026.

The codebase released is the same one that was developed, operated, and refined inside Kakao. Only minimal modifications were applied to remove security-sensitive or organization-specific details, and some internal features will be opened gradually as they are reviewed.

### Why We Open-Sourced Actionbase

**1. Transparent real-world history**

The codebase grew organically in production environments. By releasing it as-is (with only essential sanitization), we aim to share its genuine journey rather than a rewritten or polished version.

**2. Long-term sustainability**

The system has gone through multiple generations of requirements, contributors, and architectural changes. Open-sourcing it helps create a healthier long-term lifecycle and encourages community-driven improvement.
