Kakao Corporation (카카오) is a major internet platform company in Korea.

## KakaoTalk

[KakaoTalk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KakaoTalk) is Kakao's flagship messaging app and the No.1 mobile app in Korea:

- [49 million domestic MAU](https://t1.kakaocdn.net/kakaocorp/admin/ir/event/5835.pdf) (94% of Korea's population)
- 54 million global MAU
- Users open the app 50+ times a day on average

## Services

Kakao operates services across multiple verticals, holding No.1 market positions in:

- **Mobility** — Kakao T (taxi-hailing, parking, designated driver)
- **Banking** — Kakao Bank (largest digital bank in Korea)
- **Payments** — Kakao Pay
- **Webtoon** — Kakao Page (No.1 in Korea), Piccoma (No.1 globally)
- **Music** — Melon, SM Entertainment

## Actionbase at Kakao

Actionbase was developed at Kakao to handle user interactions at this scale—likes, views, follows—across multiple services. It is now open source.
